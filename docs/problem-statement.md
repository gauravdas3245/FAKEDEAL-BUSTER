
# Problem Statement

## Background

E-commerce platforms frequently promote products through sales, special offers, and discount campaigns. Customers often rely on displayed discount percentages to judge whether a product is available at a good price.

However, a large advertised discount does not always mean that the customer is receiving a genuine price reduction.

## Problem

A product may display a high discount based on an inflated or misleading original price. Customers may therefore find it difficult to distinguish a genuine price reduction from a potentially artificial discount.

Without examining relevant pricing information and historical trends, relying only on the displayed discount percentage can lead to incorrect purchasing decisions.

## Key Challenges

1. **Inflated Original Prices:** A displayed original price may make a discount appear larger than the actual price reduction.
2. **Limited Price Transparency:** Customers may not have sufficient historical pricing information to evaluate an offer.
3. **Sale-Day Pressure:** Promotional campaigns can encourage quick purchasing decisions.
4. **Manual Comparison:** Comparing prices and discount information across products and platforms can be time-consuming.
5. **Misleading Discount Indicators:** A high discount percentage alone cannot establish whether an offer is genuine.

## Impact on Customers

- Customers may believe they are receiving a better deal than they actually are.
- Purchasing decisions may be influenced by misleading discount claims.
- Comparing offers becomes more difficult.
- Trust in online promotional campaigns may decrease.

## Need for the Proposed System

A simple analytical system is needed to examine product pricing information and identify patterns that may indicate potentially misleading artificial discounts.

FakeDeal Buster aims to address this need through data analysis and machine learning-based classification.

## Proposed Approach

The proposed application will analyse available product information, including current price, original price, discount percentage, and relevant historical pricing features where available.

It will present an understandable assessment to help users evaluate a displayed discount.

## Project Limitation

The system is an academic prototype based on the selected dataset. Its predictions are analytical indicators, not definitive proof of fraudulent activity. Its reliability depends on the quality, coverage, and validity of the available data.
